Summary

  • No account or sign-in is needed to play.
  • Your game progress (wins, streaks, puzzle levels, achievements, best scores) is stored only on your device.
  • The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand usage and fix crashes, and Google AdMob to show ads. These use pseudonymous identifiers (a random install ID) - not your name or contact details.
  • The App does not use your Android Advertising ID.
  • If you choose to send feedback, your message (and email, if you enter one) is stored on our server.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored only on your device

Wins, daily streak, puzzle progress, achievements, best runner score, music preference and analytics/privacy preferences are saved locally (Android SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

Data collected by Firebase Analytics (Google)

Used to learn how the App is used and improve it. Collected automatically and through events the App sends:

  • App interactions: screens viewed, buttons tapped, game modes and options chosen, levels started/completed/abandoned, scores, time spent, power-ups collected, ad events (loaded, shown, clicked)
  • Coarse usage segments (for example "5-9 wins", "3-6 day streak")
  • Device and app information: device model, OS version, language, app version, and approximate location (country/region) derived from your IP address
  • A random per-install app instance ID

No name, email address or precise location is collected by Analytics. You can turn analytics off at any time in the App: Main menu > Privacy choices > Share usage analytics.

Data collected by Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

If the App crashes, Crashlytics receives a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, and the random install ID) so the developer can fix the problem.

Data collected by Google AdMob (Google)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob to support free development. AdMob may collect: IP address, device information (model, OS, language), and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, performance). The App has the Android Advertising ID permission removed, so it does not read your Advertising ID. Depending on your region, ads may be personalized only if you consent.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent (via Google's consent management platform) before serving ads and before enabling analytics. You can change your choice at any time in Main menu > Privacy choices.

Feedback you send us

The in-app Feedback form lets you send a message and, optionally, an email address so we can reply. We store the message, the email (if provided), the app version, the platform ("android") and a timestamp in Google Cloud Firestore. We use this only to read and act on your feedback and, if you provided an email, to reply. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message. Feedback is kept only as long as it is useful to improve the App, and you can ask us to delete it at any time (see "Your rights" below).

Where data is processed

Firebase and AdMob are provided by Google and may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Feedback submissions are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States (us-central1).

What we do NOT do

  • We do not require sign-in, registration or an account.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the Google services described above.

Your rights and choices

  • Analytics: turn off in-app at Main menu > Privacy choices.
  • Ads and consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): change or withdraw at Main menu > Privacy choices.
  • Device-level ad controls: Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Access, correction or deletion of feedback you sent (or any other personal data we hold about you): email the address below from the address you used in the form and we will delete it within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent; contact us to exercise them, or complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the App or clear its storage.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent to Google services over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The feedback database accepts new entries from the App but cannot be read back or modified by the App or other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's data practices change. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision.
